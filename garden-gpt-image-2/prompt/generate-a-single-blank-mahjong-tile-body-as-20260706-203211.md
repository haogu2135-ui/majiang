Generate a single blank mahjong tile body as a reusable game asset.

Task type: isolated product render / game prop asset.

Subject:
- One vertical mahjong tile body only.
- Front face is smooth white jade, milky white with a very subtle polished sheen.
- The visible side thickness is very thin ink-green jade, only enough to show that the tile has depth.
- The green side must read as physical thickness, not as a decorative frame or border.
- The front surface is completely blank.
- The uninterrupted blank center region must occupy at least 76% of the visible front face, reserved for later compositing of tile symbols by software.
- No complete playable mahjong face. This is only the blank body base.

Composition:
- Canvas: 1024 x 1440 portrait.
- Tile centered, mostly front-facing with a tiny 3/4 turn so the thin ink-green right or lower side is visible.
- Near-orthographic view, no strong perspective, no dramatic foreshortening.
- The full tile must be visible with clean margins.
- Tile occupies about 68-78% of canvas height.
- Pure chroma key blue background: #0000FF, flat and uniform outside the object and its tiny contact shadow.
- Opaque PNG look.

Lighting and volume:
- Soft studio light, slightly from upper left.
- Very small soft contact shadow under the tile.
- Front face remains bright and plain; side edge carries the green color.
- Fine bevel highlights on the white front, with restrained shadow at the side to show thickness.

Strict visual constraints:
- No tile face symbols.
- No Chinese characters, numbers, letters, labels, logos, watermark, calligraphy, engraving, ink marks, dots, bamboo, circles, winds, dragons, flowers, ornaments, textures that look like symbols, or decorative patterns.
- No inner frame, no inset panel, no drawn outline, no loud border, no thick green frame.
- No gold edge, no metallic trim.
- No multiple tiles, no stacked tiles, no hands, no table, no props, no scene.
- Do not crop the object.

Style target:
- Clean modern mobile game asset with subtle Chinese mahjong material reference.
- The result should feel refined and readable, with the thin green side adding depth while the blank white front stays dominant for later symbol compositing.
